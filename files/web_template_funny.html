Chub Mackerel Flasher

A 3D-printed, chrome-finished throw flasher for spearfishing. Each body prints as one piece in PLA. The head-down "wounded bait" carries a lead slug between the eyes and sealed air inside, so it spirals and flutters as it sinks. The S-curve, U-curl and straight bodies are solid plastic.
